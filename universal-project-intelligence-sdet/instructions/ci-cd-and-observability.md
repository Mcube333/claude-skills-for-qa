# CI/CD, deployment and observability

Inspect pipeline definitions, build/test stages, artifact handling, environment promotion, approvals, deployment manifests, containers, orchestration and rollback procedures.

For observability inspect logging, metrics, tracing, correlation IDs, dashboards, alert rules, error reporting and audit trails.

Document what is configured versus what is merely referenced. Identify missing or weak evidence, such as tests not included in CI or logs without request correlation. Do not trigger pipelines, deployments, cloud actions or external queries without explicit approval.

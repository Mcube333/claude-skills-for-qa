# Business workflow analysis

Derive workflows from routes, UI actions, services, state transitions, domain models, events, tests and product documentation. Clearly distinguish implemented behaviour from intended/documented behaviour.

For each workflow capture:
1. actor and business goal;
2. trigger and entry point;
3. ordered steps and components;
4. APIs/events and data changes;
5. business rules and validations;
6. success and failure outcomes;
7. alternate paths, retries and cancellation;
8. state transitions and side effects;
9. test evidence and uncovered scenarios;
10. assumptions and questions for product/engineering.

Prioritise critical user journeys and money-, identity-, privacy-, safety- or availability-sensitive flows. Do not invent business rules when code is ambiguous.

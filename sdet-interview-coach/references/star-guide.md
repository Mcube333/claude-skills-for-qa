# STAR guide for QA/SDET stories

- **Situation** (1-2 sentences): product, team, the problem and why it mattered.
- **Task** (1 sentence): what you were responsible for.
- **Action** (most of the answer): what *you* did, step by step; tools and decisions, including one you had to defend.
- **Result** (1-2 sentences): a number (defects caught, time saved, coverage, incidents avoided, release delayed/saved) and what changed afterwards.
- **Reflection** (optional, 1 sentence): what you learned or would do differently.

Aim for 90-120 seconds spoken.

Story bank to prepare (one each):
1. A critical bug caught before release (impact in money or users).
2. Automation you built that saved measurable time.
3. Pushing back on a release / saying "not ready" with data.
4. Disagreement with a developer or PM and how it was resolved.
5. A production issue you investigated (RCA) and the fix that followed.
6. A mistake you made and what you changed.
7. Learning a new tool fast (e.g. moving to Playwright, Java, k6, AI tools).
8. Improving a process (bug triage, test data, CI, analytics audits).
